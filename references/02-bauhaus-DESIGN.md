# Design System

## Brand & Style
The design system embraces a **Neutral / Modern** aesthetic, featuring clean layouts, high precision, and a sharp focus on content clarity. The aesthetic relies on crisp typography and a restrained, sophisticated color palette to evoke reliability, focus, and professionalism.

## Colors
The color palette is anchored by a deep neutral primary (`#1a1a1a`) for high-contrast text and primary actions, supported by an energetic secondary accent (`#e63b2e`) and a vibrant tertiary accent (`#0055ff`). The neutral ramp (`#4a4a4a`) provides clean structure and legibility across all surfaces.

## Typography
Typography pairs the geometric, striking character of **Space Grotesk** for headlines and labels with the highly readable, neutral qualities of **Inter** for body copy. This combination ensures maximum legibility at smaller sizes while maintaining a modern, distinctive brand voice at scale.

## Layout & Spacing
A structured grid system utilizing a standard spacing scale ensures consistent rhythm across viewports. Margins and gutters scale predictably to maintain visual balance between dense data layouts and spacious content areas.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation is achieved primarily through low-contrast outlines and subtle tonal layering rather than heavy drop shadows, aligning with the clean, flat-forward modern aesthetic.

## Shapes
UI elements feature a soft, subtle roundedness (`0.25rem` base, with larger containers scaling up to `0.75rem`), balancing structural precision with a friendly, approachable feel.

## Components
Components leverage the primary dark tone for high-impact actions, utilizing Space Grotesk labels and Inter for supportive text. Interactive states incorporate the secondary and tertiary accent colors for clear feedback.
