# Design System - Tonal Spot Nature

## Brand & Style
The design system adopts a **Tonal Spot** style, blending organic harmony with a sophisticated, contemporary feel. It evokes a sense of calm, reliability, and natural balance, making it ideal for ecological, wellness, or refined editorial platforms. The emotional tone is grounded and serene, utilizing thoughtful typography and a harmonious color palette.

## Colors
The color palette draws inspiration from nature, utilizing a gentle forest green as the primary anchor (`#4a7c59`), complemented by earthy secondary tones (`#6b6358`) and a warm accent (`#c4a66a`). Neutrals (`#4a4e4a`) provide high legibility and soft contrast against light backgrounds, ensuring an accessible and soothing visual hierarchy.

## Typography
Typography balances editorial elegance with clean readability. **Literata** is used for expressive headlines, bringing a literary and refined touch, while **Nunito Sans** serves as the body and label font, offering exceptional clarity and modern geometric neutrality across all screen sizes.

## Layout & Spacing
A flexible fluid grid system is employed, structured around a balanced 16px/24px spacing rhythm. Generous whitespace ensures that elements breathe, enhancing the natural, uncluttered aesthetic of the interface.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation is expressed primarily through subtle tonal layering and soft, low-opacity ambient shadows tinted with earthy undertones. This avoids harsh lines and maintains a gentle, tactile hierarchy.

## Shapes
Elements feature a comfortably **rounded** profile (`roundedness: 2`, approx. 0.5rem base radius for standard containers and buttons, scaling up for larger cards). This friendly geometry softens the overall interface and aligns with the organic theme.

## Components
Components inherit the rounded shape language and natural color palette. Buttons feature soft corners and clear primary/secondary states. Input fields use low-contrast outlines with subtle focus rings in the primary forest green. Cards and containers rely on tonal background shifts rather than heavy borders.
