# Vibrant Neon Dark Design System

## Brand & Style
The design system adopts a **High-Contrast / Bold** and vibrant style tailored for modern, immersive digital experiences. The visual identity relies on electric accents, deep dark backgrounds, and expressive typography to evoke energy, creativity, and technological forwardness.

## Colors
The color system is optimized for a dark mode environment (`dark`), using deep charcoal-violet neutrals (`#28283e`) combined with electric pink primary (`#ff2d78`), vibrant cyan secondary (`#00ffcc`), and energetic yellow tertiary (`#ffe04a`) accents.

## Typography
- **Headlines:** Sora
- **Body:** Inter
- **Labels:** Space Grotesk

## Layout & Spacing
A fluid grid system utilizing a consistent 8px spacing rhythm with proportional gutters and responsive outer margins.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation relies on tonal layering within the dark palette, enhanced by subtle ambient glows and minimal drop shadows.

## Shapes
Soft geometric forms with a baseline roundedness of 0.25rem, extending to 0.75rem for larger containers.

## Components
Consistent styling rules applied to buttons, inputs, cards, and navigation elements to ensure a cohesive, high-energy user experience.
