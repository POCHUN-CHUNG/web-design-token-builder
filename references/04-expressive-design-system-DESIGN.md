# Expressive Design System

## Brand & Style
The Expressive design system embraces a vibrant, high-contrast modern aesthetic. It uses bold visual statements, friendly geometries, and energetic color pairings to evoke creativity, warmth, and engagement.

## Colors
- **Primary**: `#e040a0`
- **Secondary**: `#7c52aa`
- **Tertiary**: `#0096cc`
- **Neutral**: `#604868`

## Typography
- **Font Family**: DM Sans (Headlines, Body, and Labels)

## Layout & Spacing
- Fluid grid with 1rem gutters and 1.5rem outer margins. Spacing scales from 0.25rem to 2rem.

## Elevation & Depth
- Soft tonal layering paired with low-contrast outlines and minimal shadow usage.

## Shapes
- Pill-shaped (`roundedness: 3`), ensuring fully rounded buttons and friendly component geometries.

## Components
- Styled consistently with pill-shaped radiuses, DM Sans typography, and expressive color treatments.
