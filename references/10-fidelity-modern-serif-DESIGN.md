# Fidelity Modern Serif Design System

## Brand & Style
The design system embraces a **Corporate / Modern** style fused with editorial elegance, leveraging the **fidelity** color variant to project authority, reliability, and precision.

## Colors
- **Primary**: `#3366cc` (Royal Blue)
- **Secondary**: `#54595d` (Slate Gray)
- **Tertiary**: `#fee77e` (Luminous Yellow)
- **Neutral**: `#202122` (Deep Charcoal)

## Typography
- **Headlines**: Noto Serif
- **Body**: Inter
- **Labels**: Public Sans

## Layout & Spacing
Built on a structured fluid grid with consistent outer margins and modular spacing increments to support scalable enterprise layouts.

## Elevation & Depth
Maintains clean hierarchy via tonal surfaces and restrained contrast rather than excessive shadows.

## Shapes
Soft roundedness (`0.25rem` base) for a professional yet modern interface geometry.

## Components
Includes standard accessible primitives: buttons, inputs, cards, chips, and lists styled consistently with the Noto Serif / Inter typographic pairing and royal blue primary palette.
